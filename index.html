FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking. Still unsure? Message us on Telegram anytime.

How far in advance do I need to book? expand_more

We recommend booking at least 1 to 2 weeks ahead for Silver and Golden packages, and 3 to 4 weeks for Platinum or fully custom setups, especially around weekends and holidays. That said, reach out even if your date is close. We'll always try to accommodate last minute surprises.

Can you set up in hotel rooms, restaurants, or private homes? expand_more

Yes, we regularly set up in hotel rooms, restaurants, private homes, and outdoor venues. Let us know the venue when you book so we can coordinate access with hotel or restaurant staff ahead of time.

Do you service locations outside central Addis Ababa? expand_more

Yes, we travel to surrounding areas including Bishoftu and Debre Zeit, Sebeta, and other nearby towns. A small travel fee may apply outside central Addis Ababa, so just mention your location when you reach out and we'll confirm.

What's your cancellation or rescheduling policy? expand_more

You can reschedule free of charge with at least 48 hours' notice. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the event may forfeit part of the deposit, since materials are often already purchased by that point.

Do I need to be present during setup, or can it stay a surprise for me too? expand_more

It can absolutely stay a surprise for you too. We coordinate access with a trusted third party, like a partner, family member, hotel staff, or venue manager, so the space is ready before you ever walk in.

What's actually included in the price? expand_more

Each package includes decor, setup, and cleanup as listed in the Packages section above. Cake, flowers, and balloons are included from the Golden tier up. For Silver, these can be added on request for an extra fee.

Is a deposit required to confirm my booking? expand_more

Yes, a 50% deposit secures your date and covers material costs, with the remainder due on the day of setup. This also protects you, since your slot is guaranteed once the deposit is in.

What happens if the weather is bad for an outdoor setup? expand_more

We check the forecast with you 24 to 48 hours out and offer a free reschedule, or help you shift the setup indoors, so weather never puts the whole surprise at risk.
